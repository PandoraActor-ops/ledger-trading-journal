Reading notes — "Realized" figures are reconstructed from the report's Balance column (deal-by-deal). "Floating" drawdown comes from the equity CSV's per-minute samples — it reflects unrealized loss while a position/basket was still open, which is why it's usually deeper than the realized number: price can recover before a losing basket is actually closed.
